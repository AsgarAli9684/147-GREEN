Sustainability doesn't compromise on sophistication. It enhances it.

147 Green is proudly IGBC Pre-certified: Gold, setting a new benchmark for eco-friendly, energy-efficient living. Experience sustainable luxury with reduced carbon footprint, water conservation, and healthier indoor air – all without compromising comfort or elegance.

As an IGBC Gold Pre-Certified development, every element — from solar energy to rainwater harvesting — has been thoughtfully integrated to reduce our footprint while elevating your lifestyle.
