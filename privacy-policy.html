Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the 147 Green website collects and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry through this website — including the "Schedule a Site Visit" and "Book a Visit" forms — we collect the details you provide, which may include your name, phone number, email address and the configuration you're interested in.

2. How we use your information

Information submitted through our forms is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry about 147 Green, Ulwe, Navi Mumbai
  • Share pricing, floor plans and availability updates
  • Schedule site visits and contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email regarding this project

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. How your enquiry is delivered

Enquiry submissions may be transmitted using a third-party email delivery service directly from your browser to our sales team's inbox. No enquiry data is stored on our web server.

4. Cookies

This website may use minimal, functional cookies or browser storage. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

5. Data retention

We retain enquiry details for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your interest in this project and for legitimate record-keeping, after which they may be deleted.

6. Your rights

You may request that we update or delete the information you've shared with us by contacting us using the details below.

7. Contact us

For any privacy-related questions, reach us at +91 9000000000 or admin@147green.com.
